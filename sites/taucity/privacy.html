Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Meowmin AI Diary ("we", "our", "app") is an AI-powered diary companion built by Taucity. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Diary entries — your diary text, mood check-ins, and favorites, stored locally on your device and synced to your personal Firebase account.
  • Account information — your name, email, and profile photo if you sign in with Google.
  • Usage data — anonymous analytics such as pages viewed and features used, to help us improve the app.
  • Stars and shop data — in-app rewards you earn through participation.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and sync your diary across devices.
  • To personalize your experience and generate AI insights.
  • To improve the app through anonymous analytics.
  • To send optional daily reminders if you enable them.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your diary entries are private and only accessible by you.

5. Data Security

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard security practices. Diary entries are stored in your personal Firebase project and are not accessible to other users.

6. Data Retention

Your data remains in your account as long as you use the app. You can delete your data at any time from the app's account settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Meowmin is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@taucity.xyz.
