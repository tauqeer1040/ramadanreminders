## Deep dives (fair, like the winners)

### Day One — Best polished journal (9.4)

The most carefully built digital journal, and not a close race on craft — typography, spacing, calendar view, `On This Day`, weather/location/music metadata automatically tagged. End-to-end encryption on Premium without disabling search (Journey disables search if you enable E2E). Polished Apple-first (iOS/Mac first-class, Android is second-class). The journal that survives ten years and still feels worth opening. *Best for: Apple-ecosystem users who want an archive.*

### Journey — Best cross-platform (9.0)

Genuinely everywhere — iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, Chrome OS, Web. Feature gap to Day One has narrowed in 2025 (E2E on Cloud Sync, improved prompts). 60+ coached programs (gratitude, pregnancy, fitness) + 30-day mood view. Design trails Day One, but for Android/Windows households it’s the right pick and not a compromise. *Best for: cross-platform households, Windows users, prompt lovers.*

### Reflectly — Best AI prompts (8.7)

Structured CBT rooted in positive psychology: *What happened? How did you feel? What will you do next?* Mood check-in each entry, prompts shift with your stressed vs positive streaks — helpful for blank-page paralysis, constraining for long-form writers. Now feels more chatbot-at-you than diary-for-you (Sunrise Digest’s words). *Best for: 3-5 minute guided reflection, not long paragraphs.*

### Meowmin — Best Islamic journal (for Muslims)

Write your day → get the ayah that meets it. Not scheduled verse, not random. The journal **is** dhikr. 280 characters is enough — take it slow (*habit, not hurry*). Streak shields (3 / 18 / 72 / 150 shared) hold you when life happens — no guilt leaderboard. Private, encrypted, never trains models. 114 Surahs · 120 Days (4-Month Challenge) + 30 Juz · 12 Months (Yearly) are built in, not bolted on. *Best for: Muslims who want the Quran to feel close daily.*
