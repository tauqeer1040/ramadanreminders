Welcome to Meowmin — a calm, beautiful companion for building a closer connection with Allah through daily reflection.

Writing a few honest sentences about your day is one of the simplest ways to build consistency in worship. It helps you notice your patterns, stay grateful, and keep your intentions clear.

## What Meowmin does

- **AI-powered diary** — write your day, get a personalized Islamic insight: a Quran verse, Surah story, or Hadith that matches what you lived.
- **Streaks with shields** — build a habit that survives busy days. Miss a day? A shield keeps your streak alive.
- **Shop & scratch cards** — earn stars, reveal3 daily scratch cards with guidance, and collect rewards.
- **Private by design** — your journal is encrypted, yours alone, and never used to train models.

More reflections are on the way. Start with one line tonight — your day has a verse waiting.
