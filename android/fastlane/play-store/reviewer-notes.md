# Meowmin AI Journal — Reviewer Notes
Package: `com.taucity.meowmin`  •  Category: Lifestyle
Paste relevant parts into Play Console **App content → Sensitive content / News app / Review** notes before submitting for review.

## App purpose
Meowmin is a private, on-device AI journaling app aimed at Muslims who want to reflect daily.
It offers gentle AI writing prompts, mood/reflection tracking, daily Quran verses, and prayer-time
reminders. Journal content is stored **locally and encrypted**; the app does not upload journal text to our servers.

## Login required for review?
**No login is required.** The app can be opened and used immediately (anonymous Firebase auth is used
only for crash diagnostics). Reviewers can tap **Start writing** and use every core feature without an account.

## Key flows to exercise
1. Onboarding → **Start writing** (no account needed).
2. Journal: create an entry, use an AI prompt, save it.
3. Meowmin AI: open a reflective chat; responses are generated by the in-app AI helper.
4. Today: view the daily verse and prayer times; enable reminders if prompted.
5. Premium (optional): a paywall via RevenueCat offers a subscription. Use a test purchase /
   sandbox account; do not charge a real card. Entitlement unlocks premium prompts/insights.

## Permissions & rationale
- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS** — used only for optional daily reflection and prayer reminders the user enables.
- **INTERNET** — required for Firebase (crash diagnostics, optional sign-in) and RevenueCat (subscriptions).
- **RECORD_AUDIO** — **removed**; the app does not capture audio. (No microphone usage.)
- No location, contacts, camera, or storage permissions are requested.

## Content considerations
- Journal text is user-generated and may contain personal reflections; the app does not host or publicly
  share it. No moderation queue is needed because content never leaves the device.
- Religious content (Quran verses, prayer times) is presented respectfully and for personal reflection.
